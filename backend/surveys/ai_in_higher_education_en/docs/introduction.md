# Introduction

This invented survey asked students and staff of a university about their use of AI tools in studies, teaching, research and administration. It serves as an example for the survey analysis suite: every question type and every kind of segmentation occurs once.

**Structure of the survey**
The questions cover the role of the respondents and their faculty, their satisfaction with the AI services, the tools they use, ratings of quality, priorities, and open questions about improvements and reasons.
