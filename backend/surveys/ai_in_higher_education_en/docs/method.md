# How the results were produced

## Analysis of the answers
Every question was analysed according to its type: frequencies and shares for choice questions, scale metrics (mean, median, top and bottom box) for ordered scales, descriptive statistics for numbers, and topic clusters for open answers.

## Groups of respondents
The results are also shown by role, area of study, age group and site. Differences between groups are tested; only differences that are both statistically significant and large enough are reported as findings.

## Texts written with a language model
The topic clusters of open answers and the short summaries of the findings were written with a language model and checked against the counted results. They describe the data; they do not add facts that the data do not contain.
